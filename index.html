Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

All your data stays on your device. Dumbphone stores everything locally using SwiftData. There are no analytics, no advertising, no servers, and no third-party tracking. Optional iCloud sync keeps your profiles in your private iCloud account.

1. Introduction

Dumbphone is a digital minimalism app for iOS that replaces your home screen with a text-based launcher and helps you build intentional phone habits. The app is designed with privacy as a core principle: it works 100% offline, stores your data locally on your device, and never transmits data to external servers. This privacy policy explains what data the app stores, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. What Data We Store

Dumbphone stores the following data that you create through normal use of the app:

  • Profiles: Name, accent color, active/inactive state, friction type, friction intensity, launcher appearance preferences, commitment message.
  • Schedules: Start/end times, active days (weekday/weekend/custom), profile association.
  • Launcher Items: App labels, sort order, profile association.
  • App Preferences: Active profile selection, launcher style, friction level, onboarding state.
  • Widget Settings: Font, size, spacing, alignment, text color, background style per profile.

All of this data is created by you through your use of the app. Dumbphone does not collect data from external sources or third parties.

3. Screen Time API

Dumbphone uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) to provide app blocking and friction features. Important details:

  • Dumbphone uses individual authorization (.individual), not parental controls. You block apps on your own device, for yourself.
  • App selection tokens (which apps you choose to block) are opaque identifiers provided by Apple. Dumbphone cannot see app names or bundle identifiers from these tokens.
  • Tokens are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. They are device-specific and cannot be synced.
  • Blocking configurations are managed through Apple's ManagedSettingsStore, which persists on your device.
  • No usage data is transmitted to any server. All Screen Time processing happens on-device.
  • You can revoke Screen Time authorization at any time via Settings > Screen Time.

4. How Data Is Stored

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Data is shared between the main app and its extensions (widgets, shield, monitor) through a secure App Group container on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync.

5. Optional iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your profiles and launcher items are synced to your private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit service:

  • Private Database: All synced data is stored in your private CloudKit container. Only you can access it.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.
  • The developer of Dumbphone cannot read, access, or view your synced data.
  • Screen Time tokens are NOT synced — they are device-specific and remain local.
  • iCloud sync is optional and can be disabled at any time in the app's settings.

Apple's own privacy policy applies to iCloud storage: apple.com/privacy

6. What We Do Not Collect

Dumbphone does not use or include:

  • Analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Google Analytics)
  • Crash reporting SDKs (no Crashlytics, no Sentry)
  • Advertising frameworks or identifiers
  • Location services
  • Access to contacts, photos, or camera
  • Third-party data sharing of any kind
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • User accounts, registration, or login

No data is sent to any server other than Apple's iCloud infrastructure (only if you opt into sync).

7. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete individual profiles within the app. Associated schedules, launcher items, and blocking configurations are automatically removed.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device and clears all app-blocking configurations.
  • Revoking Screen Time authorization in Settings > Screen Time removes all blocking rules immediately.
  • If you used iCloud sync, synced data persists in your iCloud account until you manually delete it through iCloud settings.

8. Wellness Disclaimer

9. Children's Privacy

2. Que datos almacenamos

Dumbphone almacena los siguientes datos que creas mediante el uso normal de la app:

  • Perfiles: Nombre, color de acento, estado activo/inactivo, tipo de friccion, intensidad de friccion, preferencias de apariencia del lanzador, mensaje de compromiso.

  • Configuracion de widgets: Fuente, tamano, espaciado, alineacion, color del texto, estilo de fondo por perfil.

Todos estos datos son creados por ti mediante tu uso de la app. Dumbphone no recopila datos de fuentes externas ni de terceros.

3. API de Tiempo de Pantalla

Dumbphone utiliza la API de Tiempo de Pantalla de Apple (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) para proporcionar funciones de bloqueo de apps y friccion. Detalles importantes:

  • Dumbphone usa autorizacion individual (.individual), no controles parentales. Tu bloqueas apps en tu propio dispositivo, para ti mismo.
  • Los tokens de seleccion de apps (que apps eliges bloquear) son identificadores opacos proporcionados por Apple. Dumbphone no puede ver nombres de apps ni identificadores de paquetes a partir de estos tokens.
  • Los tokens se almacenan localmente en tu dispositivo usando SwiftData. Son especificos del dispositivo y no se pueden sincronizar.
  • Las configuraciones de bloqueo se gestionan a traves del ManagedSettingsStore de Apple, que persiste en tu dispositivo.
  • No se transmiten datos de uso a ningun servidor. Todo el procesamiento de Tiempo de Pantalla ocurre en el dispositivo.
  • Puedes revocar la autorizacion de Tiempo de Pantalla en cualquier momento en Ajustes > Tiempo de Pantalla.

4. Como se almacenan los datos

Tus datos se almacenan localmente en tu dispositivo usando el framework SwiftData de Apple. Los datos se comparten entre la app principal y sus extensiones (widgets, shield, monitor) a traves de un contenedor seguro de App Group en tu dispositivo. Estos datos nunca salen de tu dispositivo a menos que actives la sincronizacion con iCloud.

5. Sincronizacion opcional con iCloud

Si activas la sincronizacion con iCloud, tus perfiles y elementos del lanzador se sincronizan con tu cuenta privada de iCloud via el servicio CloudKit de Apple:

  • Base de datos privada: Todos los datos sincronizados se almacenan en tu contenedor privado de CloudKit. Solo tu puedes acceder a ellos.
  • Los datos estan cifrados en transito y en reposo por Apple.
  • El desarrollador de Dumbphone no puede leer, acceder ni ver tus datos sincronizados.
  • Los tokens de Tiempo de Pantalla NO se sincronizan — son especificos del dispositivo y permanecen locales.
  • La sincronizacion con iCloud es opcional y se puede desactivar en cualquier momento en los ajustes de la app.

La politica de privacidad de Apple aplica al almacenamiento en iCloud: apple.com/privacy
